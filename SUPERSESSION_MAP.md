## 6. Clean Repo Rule

For the clean review repositories, do not include old superseded drafts unless the review assignment specifically asks the reviewer to study history.

The purpose of the clean repos is to review the current architecture, not to re-litigate old versions.

## 7. Success Standard

The supersession map succeeds when a reviewer can identify the current architecture without guessing which document controls.
