## 1. Core Doctrine

Portal equals Presentation Layer.

The Portal is not optional. The Portal is how Dispatch becomes visible, usable, and valuable to humans.

Without Portal, Dispatch cannot deliver useful value.

## 2. Purpose

The Portal transforms Dispatch activity into human-usable deliverables.

The Portal shows the right information to the right user at the right level of detail.

The Portal does not create authority. The Portal displays information, collects approved human actions, and presents clear decisions.

## 3. Mike View

The Mike view is the command cockpit.

It should include:

- Decision cards
- Review cards
- Active work queue
- Packet approval items
- Load or opportunity recommendations
- Exceptions and conflicts
- Archive and Library review prompts
- High-value operational alerts

## 4. Driver Portal

The Driver Portal supports moment-to-moment operations.

It may include assignment information, route notes, pickup and delivery details, required documents, proof photo instructions, POD package guidance, route risk notes, and check-in prompts.

## 5. Customer / Broker / Shipper Visibility

External visibility views may provide confidence-building windows, not internal system access.

External users must never receive direct access to internal databases, proprietary intelligence, raw scoring logic, or internal decision notes.

## 6. Portal and Manager

Mike does not directly operate Manager.

Mike works through the Portal.

Portal actions become structured events that the Dispatch Spine records and Manager may react to when coordination, escalation, or attention filtering is required.

## 7. Portal and Cognitive Load

The Portal exists to reduce cognitive load.

Portal presentation should be filtered by consequence:

| Level | Display Type | Human Burden |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Silent log | None |
| 1 | Status | Awareness only |
| 2 | Review | Optional inspection |
| 3 | Decision | Mike action required |
| 4 | Conflict | Mike resolution required |
| 5 | Authority | Final approval required |

## 8. Forbidden Portal Behavior

Portal must never:

- Approve work automatically
- Hide material risk
- Expose internal proprietary data to external users
- Allow external database queries
- Allow uncontrolled customer, broker, or driver access
- Replace Mike's final authority
- Display noisy low-value alerts by default

## 9. Success Standard

Portal succeeds when Mike can see what matters quickly and act with confidence.

Portal fails when the work is technically completed but not visible, usable, understandable, or decision-ready.
